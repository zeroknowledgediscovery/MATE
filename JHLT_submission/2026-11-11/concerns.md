# MATE — JHLT submission concerns (not for journal upload)

Scope: `MATE_JHLT_Final_Submission.docx` contains the title page and complete article, including abstract, main text, figure legend, and references. Figure 1 is intentionally a separate high-resolution TIFF. The cover letter is a separate DOCX. This checklist is internal only.

## Scientific verification required

1. **Cohort follow-up and index dates.** COSMOS was extracted August 11, 2026, but qualifying procedure records extend through July 22, 2026. Verify the last *eligible transplant index date*, ≥365 days of post-index observation for every included recipient, and that the dates quoted in Methods refer only to the source records. Correct the manuscript if the eligible range differs.
2. **Lung-transplant code specificity.** The cohort source list includes CPT 00580 (heart or combined heart–lung anesthesia); verify exclusion of heart-only transplantation and confirm appropriate handling of combined heart–lung cases, duplicates, and the earliest qualifying index code.
3. **Five-component phenotype selection.** The numerator 8,995/27,031 (33.28%) agrees with the state-level CSV's **MATE-EHR v1+**, which includes oxygen after day 90. This differs from earlier working MATE-EHR definitions that excluded oxygen from primary MATE. The prospective protocol should approve this as the phenotype for this paper and verify operational thresholds and component overlap.
4. **Oxygen ascertainment.** Validate whether prescription/DME codes reflect dependence (vs. single prescriptions or short-term oxygen), and whether the wording is sufficiently conservative.
5. **Geographic interpretation.** The state rates, adjusted empirical-Bayes estimates, and between-state heterogeneity are **not case-mix adjusted**; avoid inferences about center or state performance. Confirm geography is recipient residence rather than transplant center, and whether coverage varies by state.

## Author and compliance verification required

6. **IRB/ethics and data permission.** Obtain the precise IRB determination/exemption and permissions for the COSMOS analysis and confirm what statement JHLT expects in the manuscript or declarations. Do not insert an unverified approval/exemption claim.
7. **Funding and competing interests.** Confirm funding attributable to *this analysis* (the PROMISE U24 study mentioned in text is prospective validation, not necessarily support for this COSMOS analysis). Obtain each author's conflict-of-interest declaration and any Elsevier required submission form.
8. **Authors and contact details.** Confirm author order, contributions, affiliations/credentials (especially Dmytro Onishchenko's department), corresponding-author mailing address and email, and any required telephone number. Secure final approval from all authors.
9. **Data availability / reproducibility.** Confirm COSMOS data-access restrictions, availability statement, and whether JHLT requires these disclosures within the manuscript or at submission.
10. **Administrative submission declarations.** Confirm originality, no concurrent consideration, author approval, required disclosure forms and any reporting checklist through the portal. The cover letter should be approved by the corresponding author.
11. **Live JHLT portal.** Publisher author-info URL: https://www.jhltonline.org/content/authorinfo. The publisher's page was access-restricted during this review; the accessible JHLT-specific secondary guidance supports a title page at the beginning of the manuscript, an unstructured abstract ≤150 words, main text ≤1,000 words, ≤10 references, and individual TIFF/EPS figure upload. Confirm exact article type, submission forms, spacing and line-number policy in the live portal. Secondary reference: https://livewrite.app/journals/journal-of-heart-and-lung-transplantation.

**Do not upload this file to the journal.** Confirm items above before pressing Submit; any changes to science or required declarations must be reflected in the manuscript or portal, not in a reviewer comment.
